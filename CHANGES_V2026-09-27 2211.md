# V2026-09-27 2211 (called V1-0k in the app) — CIT codes on the RRA annexure, Tax & CIT page

## CIT codes — RRA-based format (option B)
- Codes = the RRA **Serial No** of the CIT annexure (CITRealAnnexure_ver7 from 2023, sheets BALANCE_SHEET and
  PROFIT_AND_LOSS) with every level on 2 digits: RRA 1.10 → `BS 01.10`, RRA 4.1 → `PL 04.01`,
  RRA 3.1.3.1 → `BS 03.01.03.01`.
- Lines the RRA file does not have are **sub-codes** of the RRA line they roll up into (decisions of 27-09-2026):
  | Old V11 | New | Rolls up into RRA |
  |---|---|---|
  | BS 3.3.1 / 3.3.2 / 3.3.3 VAT / WHT / other tax receivables | BS 03.01.04.01 / .02 / .03 | 3.1.4 Other current assets |
  | PL 4.28 Payments to subcontractors | PL 04.27.01 | 4.27 Other operating expenses |
  | PL 5.09–5.13 Other expenses, Fines, Personal consumption, Non supported, Management fees paid | PL 05.08.01–.05 | 5.8 Other administration expenses |
  | PL 6.04 Leave pay, PL 6.05 Wages of non-permanent employees | PL 06.01.01 / .02 | 6.1 Staff salaries |
  | PL 7.08 Interests from related parties | PL 07.01.01 | 7.1 Interest expense |
- **Renumbered** to follow RRA (V11 had no RRA 1.7): BS 1.07–1.10 → BS 01.08–01.11 (furniture, other assets,
  accumulated depreciation, freehold); PL 6.06–6.10 → PL 06.04–06.08; PL 8.12–8.14 → PL 08.11–08.13.
- **Added** (RRA lines missing in V11): BS 01.07 IT equipment & technologies, BS 03.03 Deferred tax assets,
  PL 09.01 Farming and livestock expenses, PL 09.02 Other extra ordinary expenses (group "Extra ordinary expenses"
  under operating expenses, note 7).
- Tax liabilities BS 07.01 → RRA 7.1. Computers → BS 01.07 (keyword rules, AI prompt, TB template example, demo;
  a warning flags computer accounts left under other assets BS 01.09).
- **Still without RRA line (to decide):** PL 02.02.02 Other direct costs (import), BS 05.09 Grants, PL 08.14
  Commissions (received) — they stay in the annual report but are not in the RRA annex.
- **Old codes are converted automatically** (TB upload, project file, V11 workbook, skill input): one digit before the
  first dot = old V11 code; a message lists the conversions and the renumbered lines. Demo data converted.
- Every code carries its RRA line (sheet, row, serial) in `data/chart_of_accounts.json`; the RRA structure and
  formulas are in `data/rra_annexure.json`.

## New page 7 · Tax & CIT
- **🧮 Taxable income**: every step with how it is computed (profit before tax, grant, each add-back with its code,
  coefficient and rule, loss brought forward and its source, tax base, rate, charge, prepayments, WHT, payable),
  warnings in red for missing inputs; **Excel with live formulas** (SUMIF on a TB sheet, inputs in blue on yellow,
  check columns against the app, sheet "Add-back rules" with a column for the legal references).
- **🏛️ RRA annex (BS / P&L)**: the statements in the RRA layout; totals by the RRA formulas (e.g. 1 = 1.1 to 1.9 −
  1.10 + 1.11; 10 = 4 − 9); sub-codes added to their parent; reconciliation with the annual report (total assets,
  gross profit, profit before tax, income tax; BS 10 must be 0); **Excel with formulas**.
- **🔗 CIT codes ↔ RRA**: the full mapping, filter, download.
- **🔄 Check an RRA annexure file**: upload a new RRA file → unchanged / new / removed / re-worded / moved lines,
  proposed updates (new codes in the same format, codes to re-link, labels), change report (Excel) and the updated
  data files for the administrator. Nothing is changed automatically.
- Explanation boxes ("📘 How … is calculated") with their own style (serif font, parchment background) and red
  warnings when elements needed for a complete calculation are missing.
