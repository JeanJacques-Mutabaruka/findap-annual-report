## Taxable income (decisions of 28-09-2026)
- **Management fees**: only `max(0, fees − 2% × turnover)` is added back; turnover = sales (codes PL 01…, RRA P&L 1).
- **Non-taxable income deducted**: dividends received PL 08.10 in full (RRA P&L 12); farming and livestock income
  PL 08.02 up to 12,000,000 (RRA P&L 13).
- **Tax losses**: entered per **year of origin** (6 years back before the first TB year + the TB years), with the part
  already used before the TB; the losses of TB years are computed from the TB when not entered. A loss is usable in the
  **5 following years** (or the maximum entered when the client has an **extension**), oldest first, never more than
  the taxable income before losses. Table "use of the losses year by year" on page 3, detail on page 7 and in the Excel
  (sheet "Tax losses"). The old derivation from retained earnings is removed.
- **Grants**: row kept at 0 (treatment to be designed); the per-year "grants" field is no longer used.
- **One place for all tax parameters**: `data/chart_of_accounts.json → income_tax` (add-back coefficients, management
  fee limit, non-taxable income and caps, carry-forward years, standard CIT rates per year, individual brackets) — used
  by the computation, the controls, the default rates and the Excel formulas (brackets included).
- **RRA line 15 vs tax base**: when losses are deducted, a warning (controls, page 7) explains that RRA 15
  (10 + 11 − 12 − 13) is higher than the tax base by the losses deducted (RRA 14) and that RRA 16 is computed after losses.
- **Depreciation**: a permanent note on page 7 — book depreciation is not yet replaced by tax depreciation (to come with
  the fixed-asset register).
- RRA annex: lines 12, 13 and 14 now filled; Excel of the taxable income rebuilt with the new steps (all ✓ on the demo).
- The Word tax note is unchanged when the new items are zero.
