  deducted, 8 million taxable).

## New page 8 · Fixed Assets
- **Template** (Excel): the three RRA "Depreciation Table" columns (description, DMC/EBM invoice, acquisition date) +
  asset ID, category (drop-down of the CIT codes), cost, disposal date and proceeds, comments; categories and rates;
  instructions; pre-filled with the current register to update it. Upload (replace or add), issues listed.
- **Register** with filters: keywords (name, asset ID, invoice, comment), categories, status, acquisition-date range,
  cost range; download of the filtered list.
- **Rates** (policy of the annual report, image of 28-09-2026): land 0%, buildings 5%, motor vehicles 25%, machinery 25%,
  intangible 10%, computers and accessories (BS 01.07) 50%, furniture and fittings 25%, office and other equipment
  (BS 01.09) 25%. **Leased property (BS 01.05) and freehold property (BS 01.11): no rate given — to enter** (warning).
  Editable per project.
- **Book depreciation**: straight-line per asset, full year in the acquisition year, **no depreciation in the disposal
  year by default** (option: full year), residual 0; gain/loss = proceeds − NBV. Life schedule of each asset from the
  acquisition to the end of the depreciation.
- **Tax depreciation (RRA)**: reducing balance per category pool, same rates; a disposal removes the asset's own tax
  value; a pool below Rwf 500,001 after the year's purchases and disposals (before depreciation) is written off at 100%.
- **Summaries** per year and category (book and tax), **book vs tax** difference (also shown on 7 · Tax & CIT and in the
  assessment — the taxable income is not adjusted yet).
- **Reconciliation with the TB** per year: cost per category, accumulated depreciation, charge, disposal result, NBV
  (red lines when different).
- **Note 11** built from the register when it reconciles with the TB for CY and PY (option: always / never).
- **RRA Depreciation Table** of a year (the 9 RRA columns, one line per asset) + Excel.
- **Excel with live formulas**: register, rates, book charge and accumulated grids, tax value and allowance grids (pool
  and 100% rule in formulas), summary by category (SUMIF / SUMPRODUCT), reconciliation, RRA table; red negatives in
  brackets.
